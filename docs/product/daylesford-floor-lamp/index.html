About Daylesford

Named for Daylesford in Victoria's spa country

Daylesford is the floor lamp for small rooms. Its shade sits at reading height and its round base slides under the edge of a chair, so the light lands exactly on the page.

Because every Daylesford is made to order in the timber you choose, no two are quite the same. The grain, figure and colour are the tree's own, so expect some natural variation from the photos.

Care

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters so the timber stays stable. A light coat of hardwax oil every few years brings back the sheen. Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician.
