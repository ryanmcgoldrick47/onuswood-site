About Bright

Named for Bright in Victoria's alpine valleys

Bright is two timber leaves standing on a shared base, each one laminated from narrow boards so the seams run the full height. A thin LED strip hides between them, so light falls in a clean band across the desk and the grain is lit from the side.

Because every Bright is made to order in the timber you choose, no two are quite the same. The grain, figure and colour are the tree's own, so expect some natural variation from the photos.

Care

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters so the timber stays stable. A light coat of hardwax oil every few years brings back the sheen. Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician.
