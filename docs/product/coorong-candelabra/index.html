About Coorong

Named for Coorong National Park, South Australia

Coorong groups turned shades on a single timber canopy, so a kitchen island gets one considered fitting instead of three separate ones. Each shade is glued up from offcuts with its seams on show, and the drops can be set at one height or staggered.

Because every Coorong is made to order in the timber you choose, no two are quite the same. The grain, figure and colour are the tree's own, so expect some natural variation from the photos.

Care

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters so the timber stays stable. A light coat of hardwax oil every few years brings back the sheen. Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician.
