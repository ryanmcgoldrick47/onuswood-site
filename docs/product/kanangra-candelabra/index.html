About Kanangra

Named for Kanangra-Boyd National Park, NSW

Kanangra is a simple open frame of four timber pieces, two long rails and two end blocks, holding a row of vertical frosted glass rods that glow evenly from end to end. It fills a double-height space or a long kitchen island with light, and the rhythm of the rods recalls the cliffs it's named for. [PLACEHOLDER: photo shows a similar fitting; Onus Wood concept render to come.]

Because every Kanangra is made to order in the timber you choose, no two are quite the same. The grain, figure and colour are the tree's own, so expect some natural variation from the photos.

Care

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters so the timber stays stable. A light coat of hardwax oil every few years brings back the sheen. Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician.
