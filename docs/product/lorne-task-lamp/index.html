About Lorne

Named for Lorne on the Great Ocean Road

Lorne sets a frosted glass mushroom shade on a rounded slip-cast ceramic base with a speckled oatmeal glaze, with a thin turned timber collar where the two meet. Switched on, the shade glows softly all over. It's small enough for a bedside table and calm enough to leave on all evening.

Because every Lorne is made to order in the timber you choose, no two are quite the same. The grain, figure and colour are the tree's own, so expect some natural variation from the photos.

Care

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters so the timber stays stable. A light coat of hardwax oil every few years brings back the sheen. Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician.
