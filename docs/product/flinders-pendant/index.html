About Flinders

Named for the Flinders Ranges, South Australia

Flinders is four timber sides joined at the corners with finger joints, left exposed so you can see exactly how it goes together. Seen from below it frames a square of light; seen from across the room it's a warm, solid block with glowing edges. Hung in a row over a bench, the joinery becomes the pattern.

Because every Flinders is made to order in the timber you choose, no two are quite the same. The grain, figure and colour are the tree's own, so expect some natural variation from the photos.

Care

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters so the timber stays stable. A light coat of hardwax oil every few years brings back the sheen. Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician.
