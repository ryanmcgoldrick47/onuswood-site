About Karijini

Named for Karijini National Park, Western Australia

Karijini is a desk lamp built around one joint. A bead-blasted cast aluminium ball joint, with a small tension knob, lets the head pivot and swivel to put light exactly where you're working. The stem and the shallow cone shade are turned from marri, the base is a flat plate of orbital-sanded aluminium, and a flush frosted opal diffuser closes the underside of the shade so you see a soft, even glow and never the bulb.

Because every Karijini is made to order in the timber you choose, no two are quite the same. The grain, figure and colour are the tree's own, so expect some natural variation from the photos.

Care

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters so the timber stays stable. A light coat of hardwax oil every few years brings back the sheen. Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician.
