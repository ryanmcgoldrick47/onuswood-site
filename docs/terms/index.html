Terms

The plain-English terms that apply when you use our website or order a piece from us. If anything here is unclear, just ask.

Who we are

Onus Wood is a small, independent Australian studio making timber lighting in Australian hardwoods. In these terms, "we", "us" and "our" means Onus Wood, and "you" means the person using our website or placing an order.

You can reach us any time at hello@onuswood.com.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or changes any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that can't be excluded. Wherever these terms limit what we're responsible for, they only do so to the extent permitted by law.

Using this website

You're welcome to browse, save pieces to your cart and send us enquiries. Please don't misuse the site, try to interfere with how it works, or use our forms to send spam or anything unlawful.

We do our best to keep the site accurate and running smoothly, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors.

Product information and images

Some of the images on our site are concept renders rather than photos of a finished piece. They show the design, proportions and finish as closely as we can, but the real thing may look a little different.

Timber is a natural material. Colour and grain will vary from piece to piece and from the images and samples you see, and timber may move slightly with the seasons. Colours can also look different on different screens. None of these differences are faults. If you'd like to see a timber in person, you can order a sample first.

Dimensions, weights, lamp types and other specifications are indicative. We confirm the final specifications with you for each order before it goes into production.

Prices, quotes and GST

All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Shipping is not included in the product price and is quoted separately for each order.

Quotes and invoices are valid for 30 days unless they say otherwise. We may change our prices from time to time, but the price that applies to your order is the one on the invoice you pay.

Mistakes occasionally happen. If there's an obvious error in a price or description, we can correct it before your order goes into production. If you'd rather not go ahead at the corrected price, we'll refund what you've paid in full.

Ordering and acceptance

At the moment, ordering works by enquiry. When you submit a cart or order request through the site, you're asking us to prepare an order for you. It isn't a binding order yet.

We'll get back to you to confirm the details, such as timber, size, finish, lead time and a shipping quote, and then send you an invoice. Your order is confirmed once we receive payment. Lead times start from that confirmation.

We may decline an order request for any reason, for example if a piece or timber is no longer available. We may also cancel a confirmed order before production starts, for instance if we find we can't make or supply it. If that happens, we'll let you know and refund what you've paid in full.

Payment

Payment is made in full against the invoice we send you, and your order is confirmed once payment is received. We may add online card checkout in the future through a payment provider such as Stripe or Shopify. If we do, your payment is handled securely by that provider and we never see your card details.

Your piece remains ours until we've received payment in full.

Made to order and lead times

Almost everything we sell is made to order for you by our specialist manufacturing partner overseas. Orders, delivery and support are all handled here in Australia.

Each product page shows an estimated lead time, typically 4 to 12 weeks. A small number of pieces are marked "ready to ship" and leave us within 2 to 5 working days. Delivery time is on top of the lead time.

Lead times are estimates, not guarantees. Making things from natural materials doesn't always go to plan, and sometimes production or freight takes longer than expected. If your order is going to be late, we'll tell you as soon as we know and give you an updated estimate. If your piece is more than 4 weeks past the lead time we quoted, you can cancel and we'll refund what you've paid in full. For shorter delays, talk to us and we'll work out a fair outcome.

Events beyond our control

Sometimes things outside our control get in the way, such as supplier problems, freight or customs delays, extreme weather, natural disasters or pandemics. We won't be responsible for delays or failures caused by events like these, but we'll keep you informed and do what we reasonably can to get your piece to you. Your right to cancel for a full refund if your piece is more than 4 weeks past the quoted lead time still applies.

Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel your order, please email us as soon as possible.

Before production starts: we're happy to update your order or cancel it and refund what you've paid in full.

After production starts: your piece is being made specifically for you, so changes or cancellation may not be possible. Where we can help, changes may cost extra, and if a cancellation is agreed we may need to keep part of what you've paid to cover materials and work already done. We'll always tell you what that is before anything is decided.

This doesn't affect your rights if a piece turns out to be faulty, or your right to cancel for a long delay. See our returns page for details.

Bespoke and custom work

We take on bespoke commissions by quote, starting from $5,000. Each project has its own quote, which sets out the scope, specifications, price, timing and payment terms.

Because a bespoke piece is designed around your space, you're responsible for confirming the details we rely on, including dimensions, ceiling heights, mounting points and whether your electrical setup is suitable. We're happy to help you check these, and your electrician can confirm the electrical side.

Once you've approved the design and production has started, changes may not be possible, and any we can make may cost extra. The approved drawings and quote form part of our agreement with you.

Trade and project accounts

We work with designers, architects, builders and other trade customers. We can agree trade or account terms with you in writing. Where a trade agreement or project quote says something different from these terms, the agreement or quote applies for that work.

Delivery and risk

We deliver Australia-wide, and internationally on request. Shipping is quoted for each order based on size and postcode, and you'll know the cost before you commit. Delivery usually adds 3 to 7 working days once your piece is made. More detail is on our shipping page.

Your piece is our responsibility until it's delivered to the address you give us. Responsibility for it passes to you on delivery.

If anything arrives damaged, please email photos to hello@onuswood.com within 48 hours of delivery. Reporting quickly helps us claim with the courier and get a repair or replacement to you sooner.

Your part in a smooth delivery

To help your piece arrive safely, please:

  • give us an accurate delivery address and contact details
  • make sure there's safe access to the delivery address
  • arrange for someone to receive the delivery where needed

If a delivery can't be completed because of incorrect details, no safe access or no one being available, or if a delivery is refused, we may need to charge you the reasonable cost of redelivery or storage.

If your piece is ready and you haven't accepted delivery or collection within 60 days, we'll contact you in writing to arrange it. If we still can't arrange delivery within a further 30 days of that notice, we may treat the piece as abandoned, to the extent permitted by law. We'll always try hard to reach you before that happens.

Installation and safety

Hard-wired lights must be installed by a licensed electrician in line with the Australian wiring rules (AS/NZS 3000). Plug-in lamps don't need an electrician.

Please follow any instructions that come with your piece, use the recommended lamp type and wattage, and don't modify the wiring. Some pieces are designed and rated for bathrooms or outdoor use, and the IP rating on each product page and spec sheet tells you where a piece can go. Unless a piece is specifically rated for it, don't use it in bathrooms, outdoors or other damp or exposed locations.

To the extent permitted by law, we're not responsible for installation carried out by others, or for loss or damage caused by incorrect installation, use in an unsuitable environment, misuse or modification. These also void our warranty.

Warranty

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

On top of those rights, we warrant timber and joinery for 10 years and electrical components for 2 years. Our warranty doesn't cover natural changes in timber colour or grain, normal wear, or damage from incorrect installation, unsuitable environments, modification, misuse or accidents.

We also offer a repair and refinish service, replacement parts and a take-back program, so your piece can keep going for a long time. Our returns page explains how to make a claim.
