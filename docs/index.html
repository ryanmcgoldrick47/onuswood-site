Every species has its own light. Every seam is on purpose.

Messmate glows pale and honey-warm. Marri carries dark gum veins like a map. Spotted gum ripples when it's lit. Blackwood goes deep and golden.

We celebrate that variety rather than hiding it. To use as much of each tree as we can, we build each light from a handful of well-chosen pieces instead of machining it from one solid block, and we leave the joins and the timber's natural marks on show. It's a more responsible use of a precious resource, and it means less of the tree ends up as waste.

Pick your timber on any product page and watch the price change with it. If you want to see it in person first, we'll post you a sample.

Designed here, made with precision

Every piece is designed in Australia by an industrial designer, then machined and turned on precision CNC and lathe equipment by our specialist timber manufacturing partner.

Machining lets us cut joints that fit tightly every time and turn shades thin enough for light to glow through the grain. It also lets us plan each cut around the board, so less of the timber ends up as waste.
