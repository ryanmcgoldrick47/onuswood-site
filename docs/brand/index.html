Brand guide

The reference for packaging, swing tags, spec sheets and anything else that carries the Onus Wood name. Concept version, November 2026.

The marks

Heartwood monogram, full. Use at 48px and up, and for branding irons 40 mm and wider. Dark SVG, light SVG.

Small version, one off-centre growth ring. Use from 24 to 48px. At 16px the W drops out (favicon).

ONUSWOOD

Horizontal lockup. Wordmark in Tenor Sans, tracked 24%, with a tightened word space. Clear space: the height of the O on every side.

Type

Light, grown slowly.

Schibsted Grotesk Medium for headings and interface. Headings set tight (-2.5% to -4.5%). Sizes: 104 / 52 / 22px on the web.

Newsreader for reading text. Warm and bookish, at 19px with generous line height, so product stories read like a catalogue, not a spec sheet.

Tenor Sans is reserved for the wordmark. Never use it for headings.

Photography

Low, warm light. Shoot pieces lit, at dusk or in dim rooms, so the timber glows. Show real grain close up. Hands and tools at the bench, never posed. No stock-photo smiles. Every current photo is a placeholder from Unsplash (see the credits file).

Tone of voice

Warm, plain and specific. Name the species, the size and the wait. Say what is true and nothing more: no "crafted with passion", no sustainability claims we can't back up, no em dashes.
