Task lamps

Task lamps are for close work: a desk, a reading chair, a workbench. They are compact and precise, with integrated LEDs chosen for accurate colour, and timber bodies you will want to keep within reach.

Choosing your timber

Every piece can be made in any of our four timbers. Messmate keeps a room light and calm. Spotted gum and marri sit in the warm middle, with ripple figure and dark gum veins. Tasmanian blackwood brings depth to darker rooms.

Each light is built from a handful of pieces, usually four to six, and we leave the joins and the timber's natural marks on show. They're deliberate, and they make each light a little different. Unsure? Order a timber sample.
