Choosing your timber

Each piece is shown with the timbers it can be made in. Paler species like Tasmanian oak and Victorian ash keep a room bright and calm. Spotted gum and blackbutt sit in the warm middle, and jarrah and Tasmanian blackwood bring depth to darker rooms.

Because we join shorter and narrower boards to make the most of every tree, you'll see seams and glue lines in the finished piece. They're deliberate, and they make each light a little different. Unsure? Order a timber sample.
