Every species has its own light. Every seam is on purpose.

Tasmanian oak glows pale and honey-warm. Spotted gum carries a ripple of darker figure that comes alive when it's lit. Blackwood goes deep and golden.

We celebrate that variety rather than hiding it. To use as much of each tree as we can, we join shorter and narrower boards instead of machining a piece from one solid block, and we leave the joins and seams on show. It's a more responsible use of a precious resource, and it means less of the tree ends up as waste.

Pick your timber on any product page and watch the price change with it. If you want to see it in person first, we'll post you a sample.
