How it works

Most bespoke pieces take 10 to 14 weeks from first conversation to installation. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm typical timing.]

  1. Consult

    We talk through the space, how it's used and the light it needs. Plans, photos and a rough budget help.

  2. Design

    We sketch options, then send drawings and a fixed quote. You see the piece at scale before anything is cut.

  3. Choose the timber

    We post samples of the species that suit the design, so you choose in your own light.

  4. Make

    Your piece is made to order. We send progress photos along the way.

  5. Install

    We deliver with fixing details for your electrician, or arrange installation for larger projects.
