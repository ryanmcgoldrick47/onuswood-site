About

Onus Wood makes lighting from Australian timber. It started with a simple idea: that a light should be as good switched off as it is switched on, and that our own hardwoods are some of the most beautiful materials in the world to put light through.

The maker

Onus Wood is led by Ryan McGoldrick, an industrial designer with years of experience designing and manufacturing furniture and lighting. [PLACEHOLDER: Ryan to write his own bio.]

That background shapes how every piece is made. We design for the workshop as much as for the room: joints that can be made well and repeatedly, shapes that suit the timber's grain, and fittings that an electrician will thank us for.

How we work

We celebrate the natural diversity and character of Australian timber. To make the most of every tree, we join shorter and narrower boards rather than machining from single solid pieces, and we let the joins and seams show. It's a more responsible use of a precious resource, and it keeps waste to a minimum.

Every piece is made to order, in the timber you choose. That means a short wait, but it also means no warehouse of unsold stock, and a light that's been made for you rather than pulled off a shelf.

Every piece is named after an Australian national park or a favourite town, the places whose forests and coastlines shaped how we think about timber and light.

We tell you exactly what you're buying: the species, the dimensions, the lamp, the light output and the lead time. If something isn't right, we'll fix it.
